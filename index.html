Satellite TLE tool

Pick the satellites you want, then write their orbit data and the time to the radio over the programming cable.

This browser can't talk to a serial port. Use Chrome or Edge, on a computer or on an Android phone with the cable on an OTG adapter.

1. Get element sets

Element sets (TLEs) are the orbit data the radio needs. They are downloaded from CelesTrak's amateur radio group when this page opens: no radio is needed for the first two steps.

Sets are kept in this browser; the page repeats the download no sooner than every two hours unless you ask.

2. Choose satellites

Tick the satellites to write. The radio holds 32.

Preview what will be written

3. Write to the radio

Plug the cable into the radio and the phone, switch the radio on, press Connect and choose the cable.

No cable was chosen, or the list was empty. The cable must be in the phone's OTG adapter with the radio switched on. Close every other tab and app that may hold the cable (the RadioFoundry page, a terminal), unplug and plug the cable in again, and try again. A phone sees an FTDI cable through Connect radio (USB cable); a cable the phone lists as a serial port (and Bluetooth serial ports) are under Other serial port.

    4. On the radio

    What the radio holds now. It is read when you connect, so you can look before writing, and again after a write.

    Advanced: retune test and diagnostics

    Retune test

    How long the receiver takes to settle after each retune, which decides how Doppler correction can be done. Receive only: nothing is transmitted. Put the radio on an FM channel or the VFO first, and use a steady FM carrier, such as a NOAA weather channel.
